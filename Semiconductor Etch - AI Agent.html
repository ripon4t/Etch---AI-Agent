Published etch research

Papers and a patent by Ripon Kumar Dey that involve etching. The agent uses them first where they apply and cites them by name. Cite the original paper, not this page.

  1. R. K. Dey, H. Ekinci, B. Cui, “Effects of mask material conductivity on lateral undercut etching in silicon nano-pillar fabrication,” J. Vac. Sci. Technol. B 38, 012207 (2020). DOI 10.1116/1.5123601

    Oxide masks (Cr₂O₃, Al₂O₃) gave roughly half the lateral undercut of metal masks (Cr, Al) in nonswitching pseudo-Bosch silicon etching with SF₆ and C₄F₈. A metal-assisted plasma etching effect is the proposed cause. Higher bias power also reduced undercut.

  2. F. Aydinoglu, F. Saffih, R. K. Dey, B. Cui, “Chromium oxide as a hard mask material better than metallic chromium,” J. Vac. Sci. Technol. B 35, 06GB01 (2017). DOI 10.1116/1.4998480

    Cr₂O₃ is easier to pattern from resist than Cr, and both gave about 100:1 selectivity over silicon in nonswitching pseudo-Bosch ICP-RIE with SF₆ and C₄F₈.

  3. H. Ekinci, R. K. Dey, B. Cui, “Two-step potassium hydroxide etching to enhance aspect ratio in trench fabrication,” J. Vac. Sci. Technol. B 37, 062001 (2019). DOI 10.1116/1.5123530

    A thermal oxide protects the trench sidewalls, RIE clears the oxide at the trench bottom, and a second KOH etch follows. This more than doubled the aspect ratio on Si(110) with almost no change in trench width. The highest (110)/(111) etch anisotropy reported was 247, with 50 wt.% KOH at room temperature.

  4. M. Samaan, H. Ekinci, R. K. Dey, X. Zhu, D. Pushin, B. Cui, “Fabrication of high aspect ratio atomic force microscope probes using focused ion beam milled etch mask,” Microelectron. Eng. 267–268, 111909 (2023).

    High aspect ratio AFM probes made with an etch mask that is milled by focused ion beam.

  5. R. K. Dey, M. Khosnegar, “Non-switching etch to fabricate the transmissive and reflective metalens for the manufacturing volume scale,” manuscript in preparation (listed for Nature Communications, 2024).

    Not yet published. The agent treats it as unpublished work and does not quote results from it.

  6. R. K. Dey, J. Shen, B. Cui, “Oxidation sharpening of silicon tips in the atmospheric environment,” J. Vac. Sci. Technol. B 35, 06GC01 (2017). DOI 10.1116/1.4998561

    Oxidation-based sharpening of silicon tips carried out in ambient atmosphere.

  7. B. Cui, R. K. Dey, “Method of fabricating nano-scale structures and nano-scale structures fabricated using the method,” US Patent 9,522,821 B2 (issued Dec. 20, 2016).

    A batch method for arrays of high aspect ratio silicon pillars. Low aspect ratio pyramid structures are etched with hard masks in place, so a pillar forms on each pyramid base. Family members listed on the inventor’s site: WO2014169383A1, CA2938783A1, CN105189821B and the Chinese utility model CN204138341U, “Silicon column array on silicon substrate.”

About the creator

I created this AI agent to support others who etch, whether you are running your first plasma recipe or tuning an advanced process. I hope it saves you time at the tool.

I’m Ripon Kumar Dey, PhD, a Principal Scientist at Deepera and a nanofabrication specialist with more than 10 years of cleanroom research experience. I earned my PhD at the Waterloo Institute for Nanotechnology, University of Waterloo, and worked there as a research scientist. I later led nanofabrication R&D teams in industry at Metamaterial Inc. and Authentix. My work covers electron beam lithography, nanoimprint lithography, thin-film deposition and etching, for photonics, sensors and displays. I have authored more than 30 peer-reviewed articles and filed more than 8 patents. I am a member of IEEE and the American Vacuum Society, and a Senior Reviewer for Microelectronic Engineering.
